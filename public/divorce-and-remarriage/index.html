Divorced and wondering about your chances?

The short answer is that they are better than the anxiety suggests. About two thirds of Americans who divorce go on to remarry, and divorce in the US has been getting less common, not more. Here is what the numbers look like for Muslims specifically.

How many American Muslims are divorced

Roughly 259,000 Muslim adults in the United States are divorced, about 9 percent of all Muslim adults. The split matters more than the total:

GroupMuslim adults
Divorced, with children185,000
Divorced, no children74,000
Divorced men146,000
Divorced women113,000

By age, divorce is concentrated later than people expect: about 40,000 divorced Muslim adults are aged 25 to 34, around 72,000 are 35 to 44, and roughly 103,000 are 45 to 59.

The remarriage numbers

There is no dedicated study of remarriage among American Muslims, so the best available evidence is the national picture from Pew Research Center:

FindingFigure
Divorced Americans who remarry66%
Divorced men who remarry68%
Divorced women who remarry64%
Divorce rate, 198022.6 per 1,000
Divorce rate, 202314.4 per 1,000

The gender gap is smaller than folklore suggests, and among adults aged 25 to 54 it has essentially closed: divorced women in that range are now about as likely as men to have remarried. The larger gap sits above 55.

What actually changes the odds

Children, far more than the divorce

Whether there are children from the first marriage is the biggest single factor in how a past marriage is weighed. About seven in ten divorced Muslim adults in the US have children, which makes a divorce without children both less common and considerably simpler.

The age range you search in

This is the lever most people get wrong. Searching among the never married in their early twenties means searching where a past marriage is most unfamiliar and where families tend to weigh it hardest. Searching in the late twenties and thirties means more of the pool has their own history, and the conversation changes completely. The pool is smaller; the reception is warmer.

How the story is told

No dataset covers this, but it is worth saying plainly: a short, calm, blame-free account tends to settle the question quickly, while bitterness or vagueness does not. Disclose it early rather than letting it surface later.

Frequently asked questions

What are the chances of getting married again after a divorce?

Good. About two thirds of Americans who have divorced go on to remarry, at 68 percent of men and 64 percent of women, according to Pew Research Center. Among adults aged 25 to 54, divorced women are now about as likely as men to have remarried.

How many American Muslims are divorced?

Roughly 259,000 Muslim adults in the United States, or about 9 percent. Around 74,000 are divorced without children and about 185,000 have children.

Does having children from a first marriage change the odds?

It is the single biggest factor in how a past marriage is weighed, far more than the divorce itself. Around seven in ten divorced Muslim adults in the US have children, so a divorce without children is the less common and less complicated case.

Is divorce becoming more common in America?

No, it is becoming less common. The US divorce rate fell from 22.6 divorces per 1,000 married women in 1980 to 14.4 in 2023, according to Pew Research Center.
