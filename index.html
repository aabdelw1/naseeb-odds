What is Naseeb Odds?

Naseeb Odds is a just-for-fun Muslim marriage odds calculator. It starts with every Muslim in the United States and narrows the pool as you add what you're looking for: brothers or sisters, age, ethnicity, height, marital status, education, income, prayer, sect and more. Every filter shrinks the circle of people, so you can see how rare your perfect match really is.

How the numbers work

Instead of multiplying separate percentages, Naseeb Odds models US Muslims so that traits go together the way they do in real life: high earners are mostly degree holders, immigrants are more often married, and young women are more likely than young men to have finished college. The model is tested against every published figure it uses. The results are rough estimates for fun, not facts about any real person.

Frequently asked questions

How many Muslims live in the United States?

Estimates range from about 3.45 million (Pew Research Center, 2017) to 4.5 million (US Religion Census, 2020), with some newer estimates higher. Naseeb Odds uses 4.5 million by default, and the Conservative and Generous settings show the range.

Is Naseeb Odds a matchmaking or rishta app?

No. It doesn't collect profiles or introduce anyone. It's a calculator that estimates how many people match a set of preferences, meant for fun and conversation.

Where do the numbers come from?

Pew Research Center surveys of US Muslims (2017 and 2023–24), the ISPU American Muslim Poll (2025), the US Religion Census (2020), the US Census Bureau and National Center for Education Statistics, CDC height measurements, and Bureau of Labor Statistics earnings data. Traits are modeled together, so combinations stay realistic.

Can I share my search?

Yes. Your filters are saved in the page address, so you can copy the link or use the Share button to send the exact search to friends and family.
